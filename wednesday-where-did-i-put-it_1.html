Where Did I Put It?

Daily practice

You had them ten minutes ago. You’ve been in four rooms since. Now retrace it.

A few everyday things get left around the house. Learn where each one ended up, take a short break, then say which room you left it in — twice over.

Four takes about three minutes.

Walk through it in your head, room by room. Picturing yourself putting each thing down works better than staring at the page.
