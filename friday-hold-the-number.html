Hold The Number

Daily practice

Somebody reads out a number. You have to keep hold of it just long enough to use it.

A number appears, then disappears, and you tap it back in. Each one you get right, the next is a digit longer. Halfway through, you’ll be asked to give them back the other way round — last digit first.

The shortest game of the week — about two minutes.
